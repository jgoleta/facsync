Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 27, 2026

1. About FacSync

FacSync is a non-commercial student capstone project operated by the FacSync student project team at Ateneo de Naga University. It helps students, faculty and college administrators coordinate faculty availability, schedules, consultations and walk-in queues. It is an academic project, not a commercial company.

2. Information we collect

When you sign in with Google, we receive basic account information, including your name, email address, Google account identifier and profile information returned by Google, which may include a profile-picture URL and email verification information. We use this information to identify your account and provide access to FacSync. FacSync does not receive your Google password.

We also store information provided through registration and normal use: your username, role, college affiliation and account status; student ID and year level where applicable; faculty ID, office location and biography; consultation requests, agendas, messages and faculty notes; schedules; availability and status history; walk-in queue records; faculty-status subscriptions; and administrative records such as invitations, announcements and closure notices.

We keep account and activity timestamps, notifications and read status, and technical information needed for account access and synchronization, including event identifiers and synchronization errors.

3. How we use and display information

We use this information to operate accounts, display faculty availability and schedules, arrange consultations, manage walk-in queues, deliver notifications, support college administration and generate operational analytics.

Faculty names, college affiliations, availability, status notes and update times are visible on the public availability dashboard. Signed-in students can view faculty schedules. Consultation and queue information is used by the participants and administrative features that support these services.

FacSync does not sell Google user data, share it with advertisers or use it for advertising. The project uses it for the account, scheduling, communication and analytics functions described here. Service providers process information as described below.

4. Optional Google Calendar connection

Faculty members may connect Google Calendar separately from Google Sign-In. When connected, FacSync requests permission to read, create, update and delete calendar events to synchronize schedules, check conflicts and update availability. The connection uses the faculty member's primary calendar and currently reads a window from one day in the past to sixty days ahead.

FacSync stores selected event details locally, including titles, descriptions, dates, times, event types and event identifiers. Imported schedule titles and descriptions can appear in student-facing faculty schedules. Please avoid connecting a calendar containing details you do not want displayed through those schedules.

For synchronized consultations, FacSync sends Google the student's name and email address, appointment details, request identifier and faculty note. Google may send invitations and event updates to the student.

We store access and refresh tokens, expiry information and synchronization timestamps to maintain the connection without requiring repeated sign-in. The synchronization window is not a deletion deadline for locally stored records.

5. Service providers and analytics

Google Sign-In and Google Calendar API: Google processes information for account authentication and optional Calendar synchronization.

Brevo: We use Brevo for transactional email delivery. Brevo processes recipient email addresses and email content, such as invitations, account notices, availability updates, announcements, schedule-upload details and consultation or walk-in notices.

Google Gemini API: We send selected aggregate college analytics to Gemini to generate explanations and planning insights. These summaries include college and reporting-period information, counts and trends, and can include availability statistics derived from synced calendars. FacSync removes identifying and credential fields from the analytics payload and does not include faculty names or raw Calendar titles and descriptions in the schedule summary. Generated insights are stored for later display and may be inaccurate.

Google's handling of Gemini submissions depends on the service configuration. Under its unpaid-service terms, Google may use submitted content and generated responses to improve its products and machine-learning technologies, and human reviewers may process them. Paid-service terms provide different data-use protections. We have not confirmed paid-service protection for this deployment, so users should not assume these submissions are excluded from Google's product or model improvement. See the Gemini API terms.

Vercel and Supabase: Vercel hosts the application, and Supabase provides database storage. These providers process application traffic and stored records as needed to operate the service.

6. Retention and account removal

FacSync currently has no automatic deletion period for ordinary account, consultation, schedule or college records. These records remain in the system until removed through available application functions or by an administrator. Deactivating an account preserves its records. An announcement's expiry controls its display rather than deleting the stored record.

You may request account removal through your College Head, the FacSync Super Admin or facsync@gmail.com. Account deletion can also remove related records. It does not automatically remove emails already delivered or events retained in Google Calendar, or automatically revoke Google access. Provider-held logs and backups are subject to the respective provider's retention arrangements; FacSync does not promise immediate deletion of those copies.

You can disconnect Google Calendar in FacSync. This removes the locally stored Calendar credentials and linked schedule records, clears consultation synchronization links and attempts to revoke Google access. Consultation records remain, and disconnecting does not delete events already held by Google. You can also revoke access through your Google Account settings.

7. Security and cookies

FacSync uses account authentication, role-based permissions and secure connection settings to help protect information. Session and security cookies support sign-in and protect requests. We do not use advertising trackers. No system can guarantee complete security.

8. Contact and changes

For privacy questions, corrections, inquiries or account-removal requests, email the FacSync project team at facsync@gmail.com. You may also contact your College Head or the FacSync Super Admin.
